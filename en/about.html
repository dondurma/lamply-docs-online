About Lamply

Effective: 2026-11-25

Lamply is a scheduling and lesson-fee workspace for independent teachers: see what you teach today, how the week is arranged, how many lessons each student has left, and what you received this month—ideally in one calm place.

Why we built it

Independent teachers often assemble a workflow from calendars, notes, and spreadsheets, with records scattered across tools. Lamply brings that into a quiet, local-first app: less setup, more certainty.

No Lamply account and no Lamply servers are required; there are no ads or third-party tracking. Data stays on this device by default and syncs across your devices through Apple iCloud after you sign in.

Version and devices

Version 1.0.0; supports iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or later; available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English.

Membership and pricing

Lamply will be offered commercially on the App Store, with monthly/yearly auto-renewing memberships and a lifetime membership. Benefits, prices, and availability will be shown on the App Store product page. The current version does not enable purchases.
