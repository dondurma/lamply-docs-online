User Guide

Effective: 2026-11-25

This guide covers the main path from adding a student to collecting tuition. The “+” button usually means add.

1. Add a student and package

Students → “+” → enter name, source, level, phone/notes → choose a billing mode, price, and package under “First account” → save.

From the student detail you can then add a package, record a payment, or schedule a lesson.

2. Schedule a lesson

Tap “+” on the Schedule page, or tap an empty slot in the week view to bring in the date and start time. Choose the student, duration, and location, then save. Overlapping times are highlighted and saving is blocked.

Tap an existing lesson to change its time, duration, or location, or to mark leave/cancel.

3. Fixed schedule

Student detail → “Add fixed schedule” → choose weekday, start time, weekly/every other week, and end date. Lessons are generated through the end of the term, skipping existing lessons and conflicts.

4. Check-in, leave, and make-ups

“Check in” on Today deducts the lesson and records the fee; you can undo within five seconds. Leave does not deduct and creates a make-up credit. Absence follows your default or a one-off charge/waive/postpone choice. Arrange make-ups from “Make-ups” in the student detail.

5. Tuition and monthly statements

Tuition shows payments and lesson usage by month, filtered by private/music school. “+” adds a one-off charge; “Monthly” generates statements for monthly accounts; unpaid statements can be copied as a payment reminder.

6. Settings and data

Open Settings from the gear on Today to set your name/avatar, currency, absence/make-up/renewal defaults, default duration, common packages, settlement day, make-up month, terms, week start (Monday/Sunday), lesson reminders, and iCloud sync status. You can also export a JSON backup or clear business data while keeping your profile and settings. After you sign in to iCloud, data syncs across your devices; without iCloud it stays on this device, and deleting the app clears local data.

7. Membership and purchases

Once Lamply is available commercially, it will offer monthly/yearly auto-renewing memberships and a lifetime membership. See the App Store product page for benefits and prices. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account; deleting the app does not cancel. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase. A Restore Purchases option will be available when membership launches.
