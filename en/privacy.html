Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-25

Lamply (“we”) is a scheduling and lesson-fee record-keeping tool for independent teachers. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Where your data lives

This version is local-first. Student, lesson, package, payment, and note data you enter is stored in the app’s local database on this device. This version does not require an account, does not connect to our servers, and does not enable iCloud sync.

Deleting the app removes local data. Device backups (for example iCloud or computer backups) are managed by Apple or the backup tool you choose and are not Lamply servers.

2. What we collect

We do not collect or upload your student records, contact details, lessons, or accounts. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs.

To schedule lessons and keep records, the app processes information you choose to enter on this device: student names, optional phone numbers and notes, lesson times, packages and prices, payments, and preferences.

3. System permissions

This version does not request contacts, photos, location, microphone, or notification permissions. If a future version asks for notifications for reminders, it will explain why first; you may decline and keep using the core recording features.

4. In-app purchases

This version does not offer in-app purchases or auto-renewing subscriptions. If we add them, Apple will process payment; we will not receive your card details and will only check whether a transaction is valid to unlock the relevant entitlement.

5. Sharing, transfers, and sale

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. This version has no server, so Lamply does not transfer your data across borders.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, delete the app, or erase the device. Because this version has no server copy, we cannot remotely restore or delete data on your device.

7. Children and student information

Lamply is intended for teachers and adult users, not children. When you enter student information, you are responsible for having the right to record it and for complying with local rules on minors’ personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the effective date when features or data practices change. We will give notice in the app for material changes.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact ruanweidev@163.com.
